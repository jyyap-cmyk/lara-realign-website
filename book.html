Questions

Is this tied to one religion or belief system?

No. The book pulls from quantum physics, neuroscience, and philosophy across multiple traditions, Eastern and Western both. It connects dots that science and ancient thought have both been pointing at separately, no faith required.

Do I need a background in science or spirituality to understand this?

No. The science is explained in plain language, no jargon, no equations. If you've ever wondered why things happen the way they do, that's the only prerequisite.

What if I don't believe in things like karma or past lives?

You don't have to walk in believing anything. The book builds each idea from evidence first, belief second. Several chapters work even if you only accept the physics and leave the rest as a question mark.

Am I too old to change?

No. Every present moment gives you the power to decide differently. Age has nothing to do with it.

What if I'm scared of what I'll find out about myself?

That fear is common. Your brain is wired to protect you, and the familiar always feels safer than the unknown, even when the familiar is what's keeping you stuck. The discomfort means you're doing something right.

Is this information dangerous?

No, but it could shift your whole reality. If you're happy where you are and not ready to know more, this isn't for you.
