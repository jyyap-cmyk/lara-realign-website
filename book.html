From the preface

Who this is for

Written for you if

  • You have been asking why you are here, and the answers around you stopped satisfying a long time ago.
  • You think in evidence. You want the research, not a request to simply believe.
  • From the outside things look fine, and something still feels unfinished.
  • You have read the self-help and found it worked on the surface only.

Probably not for you if

  • You want a quick fix or a five step morning routine.
  • You would rather not have your assumptions about reality touched.
  • You are looking for religion. This is not one, and it does not ask you to join anything.

Why I wrote it

After 11 years of researching consciousness and the psychology of the mind, I wrote the book my younger self wished had existed.

I have always been a rational thinker who loves science and maths, and I enjoy fitting the puzzle together until every piece has a place. So this is drawn from science, philosophy, and ancient wisdom, footnoted where it needs to be, and non religious throughout. It is for the ones who were always meant to know.
